Themis Barbeque House

Themis Barbecue House is a lively multi-cuisine restaurant known for its unlimited barbecue buffet, live grilling experience, and extensive food spread. Its menu features North Indian, Mughlai, Italian, and Asian cuisines, with a variety of vegetarian and non-vegetarian starters, main courses, live cooking counters, and desserts. With Greek-inspired interiors, rooftop seating, and live music, it is an ideal destination for family outings, birthday celebrations, parties, and group dining.
