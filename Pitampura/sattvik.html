Sattvik Restaurant

Sattvik is an upscale vegetarian restaurant offering a contemporary dining experience with a focus on regional Indian cuisine. Its menu features traditional Indian dishes with a modern twist, including chaats, kebabs, paneer delicacies, curries, and biryanis. With its elegant ambience and family-friendly setting, it is suitable for family dinners, celebrations, and special occasions.
