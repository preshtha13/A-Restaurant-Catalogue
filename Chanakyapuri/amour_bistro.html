Amour Bistro

Mediterranean venue where the decor and menu both take their inspiration from Europe. Spanning the coast of the Mediterranean, the rustic cuisine here while at heart stays very much European, one can find flavors from around the globe to enhance the dining experience in line with the latest trends in World cuisine and dining. The famous Amour wood fired pizzas and desserts make their way at the bistro as well.
