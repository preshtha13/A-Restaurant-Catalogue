Megu - The Leela Palace

As one of the most sought-after Japanese restaurants in the city, Megu offers modern Japanese delicacies to its patrons amidst a traditional Japanese setting. The restaurant has also been recognized amongst Asia's 50Best Restaurants in 2022 and World's 50 Best Discovery in 2024. Megu follows an East-Asian theme, seamlessly melding modern design elements with traditional Japanese motifs and hues to create a luxuriously contemporary setting.
