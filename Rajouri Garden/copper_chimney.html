Copper Chimney

Indulge in an exquisite culinary journey at Copper Chimney, nestled in the heart of Rajouri Garden, New Delhi. Savor the rich flavors of North Indian and Mughlai cuisine, featuring delectable biryanis, succulent kebabs, and delightful desserts. Perfect for fine dining and cozy get-togethers, our charming ambiance and refreshing juices make every meal unforgettable.
