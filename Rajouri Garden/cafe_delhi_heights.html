Cafe Delhi Heights

Lively cafe featuring live music and fusion cuisine including burgers, pizza, and pasta. The only motto at Cafe Delhi Heights is serving our customers with good food. It is a great place to hang out with friends, have smashing birthdays' or electrifying parties, to tap your foot with our in-house DJ or enjoy Live Band performances while you toss great food in your mouth. It's a place which serves all your needs.
