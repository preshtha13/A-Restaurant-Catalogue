Elia

North Indian restaurant with rooftop terrace and versatile space for social events. This stylish lounge in West Delhi serves a wide range of veg and non-veg dishes, including North Indian, Continental, Chinese, and Italian cuisine. Elia Kitchen and Bar Rajouri Garden is popular for its live music, DJ nights, and elegant interiors, making it a top choice for parties and celebrations. With rooftop seating, a full bar, and modern decor, it creates a lively yet comfortable atmosphere.
