Sandoz Restaurant

A casual-dining, multi-cuisine restaurant known for its North Indian delicacies, Mughlai preparations and Chinese dishes. It offers a comfortable dining environment with an extensive menu featuring kebabs, tandoori starters, curries, biryanis and vegetarian specialities. It is suitable for family dinners, group outings and casual gatherings.
