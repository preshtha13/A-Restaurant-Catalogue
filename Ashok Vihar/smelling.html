Smelling Salts

An upscale-casual, pure-vegetarian restaurant known for its extensive multi-cuisine menu and elegant interiors. It specialises in North Indian and Mughlai-inspired vegetarian dishes, while also offering Chinese, Continental and Oriental preparations. With comfortable seating and a family-friendly atmosphere, it is suitable for family dinners, group outings and celebrations.
