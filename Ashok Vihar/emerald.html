Emerald House

A rooftop restaurant and lounge in Ashok Vihar, known for its relaxed social atmosphere, outdoor seating and diverse multi-cuisine menu. It serves a combination of North Indian, Chinese and Continental dishes, along with Japanese-inspired options, pizzas and pastas. Its rooftop setting makes it suitable for casual outings, family dinners, group gatherings and small celebrations.
