Sufiaana

A contemporary, premium multi-cuisine dining restaurant in Ashok Vihar, Delhi. Its menu features a variety of international and Indian-inspired dishes, including Asian, Continental and fusion options. It offers an upscale setting suitable for family dinners, social gatherings and celebrations. Its menu includes dishes such as avocado toast, falafel pita pockets, Korean-style cottage cheese and mezze platters.
